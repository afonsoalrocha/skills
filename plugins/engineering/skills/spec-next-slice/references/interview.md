# Short interview

Objective: close the decisions for the next slice, not for the whole project (source: Matt Pocock's grill-with-docs skill, shortened).

- One question at a time, starting with the one that most changes the outcome.
- For each question, offer the recommended answer and the reason. The owner confirms or corrects it.
- If the answer is in the code, the documents, or memory, look it up instead of asking.
- Stop when no open decision remains that would change the next slice. Questions about future slices go on the overall plan as "open".
- Put new domain terms into `GLOSSARY.md` immediately (see the `design-architecture` skill).
