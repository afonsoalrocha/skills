# Glossary and decision records

`GLOSSARY.md`: a term, a one-line definition, and the synonyms that are forbidden. Humans, agents, and code use the same names (Pocock).

A short ADR at `docs/adr/NNNN-title.md`, only for a decision that is hard to reverse or that would surprise someone:

```text
# NNNN Title
Context: <why a decision was required>
Decision: <what was chosen>
Alternatives: <what was rejected, and why>
Consequences: <what becomes easier, and what becomes harder>
```
