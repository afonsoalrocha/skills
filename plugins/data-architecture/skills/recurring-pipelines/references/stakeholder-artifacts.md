# Jobs that generate slides, PDFs or documents

- Separate content, artifact model, visual template and rendering.
- Reuse the project's official templates; no colors, fonts or coordinates scattered through business rules.
- Validate totals, grouping, order, links and required fields before saving.
- Render and inspect every relevant page/slide for clipped text, overlaps and overflow.
- Keep a human preview step before sending or presenting.
